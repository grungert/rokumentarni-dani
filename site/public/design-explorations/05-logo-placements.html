Internal · Logo placement research

Where should the animated logo live?

Five concrete experiments. Each is interactive — try them, then tell Claude which to wire into the real site (or which combination).

01 · Boot loader on first load

Pixel cells light up one-by-one when the page first loads — like a film projector warming up — then settle into the still mark. Plays once per visit (sessionStorage), respects reduced-motion, dismisses on click. Replays via the button.

02 · Poster placeholder in film grids

Each film card uses the animated logo as its poster fallback. While you wait for an image to load — or for stills you don't have yet — the logo plays. Live cells animate independently per card. The logo doubles as a watermark on real posters (small mark in the corner).

03 · Interactive cursor field hover the logo

Cells closest to your cursor brighten and lift; cells far away fade. The logo becomes a tactile object. Stays still when the mouse leaves. On touch devices it falls back to the gentle twinkle.

04 · Audio EQ music visualizer

Bottom rows pulse rhythmically like an audio meter. The rock-hand becomes a "now playing" indicator — works as a small floating mark when a video is playing on the page, or as a hero accent. Loops at a fixed BPM (no real audio yet — easy to wire to a trailer's AudioContext later).

05 · Hover-only header opt-in twinkle

Logo in the header is dead-still by default. Hover over it (or any part of the header) and cells start dancing. Returns to still when you move away. Zero ambient motion, signature reveal — feels like the page noticed you.

06 · Cell hover glow touch any brick

Move the cursor over individual cells — each one lights up with a glow halo (drop-shadow + brightness) as you pass over it. Tactile, arcade-button feel, leaves a brief trail. Pure CSS — runs at full framerate, no JS bookkeeping.

07 · Constellation draw your own — game

Pick a colour, drag across the grid to paint. Toggle between ✦ glow and □ fill-only. Use ⌫ to erase, then save your drawing as a PNG to share.
